    1. Welcome Notification

    Sent automatically to a new employee the moment their user account is created in PM365. This is the very first email a new hire receives from the system.

    1. Welcome Notification

    Trigger: An Admin/HR user opens Administration → Users → Add User, fills in the new employee's details, and clicks “Save and Notify” (instead of a plain “Save”).



    Add User form — clicking “Save and Notify” (highlighted) creates the account and fires the welcome email.

    2. Self Review

    Covers the stage where the employee evaluates their own performance. All the date-based reminders in this module are calculated from the “Self Review Start Date” and the number of days configured for Self Review on the Appraisal Cycle.

    1. Self Review Announcement (1 day before Self Review starts)

    Trigger: Calculated automatically from the Appraisal Cycle setup — specifically the “Self Review Start Date” field. One day before that date, this heads-up email goes out.



    Appraisal Cycle setup — “Self Review Start Date” and “No. of Days for Self Review” drive this and the next 3 templates.

    Email Template:



    ⚠ Power Automate required: This email fires automatically on a calculated date (e.g. “1 day before”, “on the start date”, “on the last day”). PM365 does not have its own internal scheduler for these date-based sends, so a Power Automate scheduled flow (a daily/recurring trigger that checks the relevant dates and calls PM365) must be configured to actually send it.


    2. Self Review Notification (on the start date)

    Trigger: Fires automatically on the Self Review Start Date itself, telling employees the review window is officially open.

    Email Template:



    ⚠ Power Automate required: This email fires automatically on a calculated date (e.g. “1 day before”, “on the start date”, “on the last day”). PM365 does not have its own internal scheduler for these date-based sends, so a Power Automate scheduled flow (a daily/recurring trigger that checks the relevant dates and calls PM365) must be configured to actually send it.


    3. Self Review Last Day Reminder (1 day before it closes)

    Trigger: Fires one day before the Self Review End Date, nudging employees who haven't submitted yet.

    Email Template:



    ⚠ Power Automate required: This email fires automatically on a calculated date (e.g. “1 day before”, “on the start date”, “on the last day”). PM365 does not have its own internal scheduler for these date-based sends, so a Power Automate scheduled flow (a daily/recurring trigger that checks the relevant dates and calls PM365) must be configured to actually send it.


    4. Self Review Last Day Notification (on the last day)

    Trigger: Fires on the actual last day of the Self Review window.

    Email Template:



    ⚠ Power Automate required: This email fires automatically on a calculated date (e.g. “1 day before”, “on the start date”, “on the last day”). PM365 does not have its own internal scheduler for these date-based sends, so a Power Automate scheduled flow (a daily/recurring trigger that checks the relevant dates and calls PM365) must be configured to actually send it.


    5. Self Review Submission Notification (to the employee)

    Trigger: The employee fills in Achievements / Self Reflections / Action Plan / Summary, ticks the terms & conditions box, signs their name, and clicks “Submit & Sign.” PM365 immediately emails them a confirmation copy.



    Employee's Self Review form — Achievements, Self Reflections, Action Plan, Summary tabs.



    Employee accepts terms, signs, and clicks “Submit & Sign.”

    5. 360 Feedback

    Lets an employee's peers/manager give rounded, multi-perspective feedback outside of the formal appraisal cycle.

    1. Request Feedback (to the person being asked)

    Trigger: Someone opens 360 Feedback → Feedback Request(s) → “+ Request,” picks who the feedback is from, chooses a template, and clicks Submit.



    “Request Feedback” panel — choose who it's requested from and which template to use

    Email Template:



    2. Feedback Reminder (to the person who hasn't responded)

    Trigger: From the Feedback Request(s) list, clicking the envelope/“Reminder” icon next to a pending request nudges that person.



    “Feedback Request(s)” tab — the “Reminder” icon on a pending request.

    Email Template:



    3. Feedback Completion (to the original requester)

    Trigger: The person being asked fills in the star ratings and comments and clicks “Submit.” The requester is then emailed to say it's done.



    “Give Feedback” form — ratings and comments, then Submit.

    Email Template:



    4. Email To (sharing a Feedback Report)

    Trigger: An HR Admin opens Report → Feedback Report, views the details for someone, and clicks “Email To” to send the summarized ratings out.



    “Feedback Report” screen — filter by date/employee and click “View.”



    “Feedback Details” — the “Email To” button sends the summary.

    6. 1:1

    Covers one-on-one meetings between a manager and their reportee, plus the follow-up action items that come out of them.

    1. Schedule a Meeting (to both participants)

    Trigger: A 1:1 is created — title, template, participants, date/time — and saved. Both people get an email confirming the details



    1:1 scheduling screen — meeting details, talking points, and action points.

    Email Template:



    2. Meeting Overdue (to the employee)

    Trigger: There is no in-app button for this one — it needs to fire automatically once a scheduled 1:1's date has passed and the meeting is still marked incomplete.

    Email Template:



    ⚠ Power Automate required: This is the specific template you flagged: PM365 has no built-in scheduler to detect “meeting date has passed and still incomplete.” A Power Automate flow needs to run on a schedule (e.g. daily), check for overdue 1:1s, and trigger this email.


    3. Task Reminder (manual nudge to a task owner)

    Trigger: From 1:1 → Team Tasks, clicking the envelope icon on an overdue task and confirming “Do you want to send Reminder?”



    1:1 scheduling screen — meeting details, talking points, and action points.

    Email Template:



    4. Task Notification (when a task is first assigned)

    Trigger: When an Action Point in a 1:1 is assigned to someone (with a due date), that person is emailed right away.

    Email Template:
